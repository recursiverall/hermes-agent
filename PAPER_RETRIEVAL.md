# Paper Retrieval Policy

**Mandatory default for scholarly paper retrieval in this research repository.**

Use [Paper Pilot](https://github.com/aytzey/paper-pilot) as the **first route for every attempt to locate, obtain, inspect, or cite a scholarly paper** whenever the current environment can run or reach it.

1. Resolve/search through Paper Pilot before creating ad-hoc paper downloaders or manually hopping among scholarly sites.
2. For material claims, prefer the full paper over title/abstract snippets; follow all continuation cursors needed to read the relevant pages and inspect relevant figures/tables.
3. Record DOI/stable identifier, title, provider/access status, and supporting PDF page(s) when available.
4. A Paper Pilot miss, provider outage, MCP failure, or network failure means **access unresolved**, not “paper/evidence absent.” Continue through authorized fallbacks such as publisher OA copies, arXiv, bioRxiv/medRxiv, PubMed Central/Europe PMC, author/institutional repositories, institutionally authorized copies, or a user-provided PDF.
5. Already-provided/local PDFs need not be re-downloaded; import/read them through Paper Pilot when practical for consistent page citations, otherwise inspect them directly with provenance retained.
6. Do not make research depend on Paper Pilot availability. If it cannot run in the current environment, state that boundary and use the best authorized fallback.
7. Paper Pilot's optional Sci-Hub/LibGen integrations are **not** part of this default policy; use its open-access/authorized provider path unless a separate applicable policy explicitly establishes another lawful source.
